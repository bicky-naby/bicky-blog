No, like **actually** sit down because i have been THROUGH something this week and it has changed me on a cellular level. like i am literally a different person now. i have been **investigating** 🕵️‍♀️ the 1992 U.S. Women's Open which was held at this place called Oakmont Country Club in Pennsylvania, and i thought — oh cute 🌸, a golf course where women won things, iconic, let me look into it 🌸⛳✨

and then i found out it's a **SECRET SOCIETY**.

i know this sounds silly, but like… hear me out. i went to their website 🌐 and it was just a picture of a hedge. A HEDGE. no contact info, no "join us," just a hedge that looks like it's judging you 🪴😒. and then i found an old forum post from 1992 🗓️ that said "if you make a sound during the Women's Open you will be escorted out by men in green blazers" and i was like WAIT 👀 THAT'S A RULE???

so obviously i applied. i filled out a form that i found on a pdf from 1993 that someone left on a geocities page about lawn mowers. i wrote: "reason for joining: i saw a grass blade and screamed" and "signature: BICKY NABY (in glitter pen)" and then i mailed it to the hedge address with a stamp of a ladybug 🐞

and this morning i got a letter back. it's on cardstock that smells like old money 💸 and regret. i'm gonna show you the exact text because it's THE FUNNIEST THING I'VE EVER RECEIVED.
