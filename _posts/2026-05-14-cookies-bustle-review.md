  <h3 style="color: #FFFF00; text-align: center; text-shadow: 1px 1px 2px #000;">
    🎮 WHAT EVEN *IS* COOKIE'S BUSTLE?! 🎮
  </h3>
  <p style="color: #00FFFF; font-family: 'Comic Sans MS', 'Comic Sans', 'Chalkboard', 'Chalkboard SE', 'Marker Felt', fantasy, sans-serif; line-height: 1.6;">
    Okay, so like, <strong>Cookie's Bustle</strong> is this *super* rare point-and-click adventure game made by this tiny 8-person indie studio in Japan called RODIK!!! 🇯🇵✨ It came out in 1999 (yes, the *same year* as *NSYNC's *No Strings Attached* album, *coincidence*? I THINK NOT!!! 🎤) and it was *only* released in Japan at first!!! 
    <br><br>
    The game follows our *iconic* heroine, <strong>Cookie Blair</strong> (yes, that's her *actual* name and it's *perfection* 💅), a *5-year-old girl from New Jersey* who *thinks* she's a *teddy bear*!!! 🧸💖 She goes on this *wild* adventure in a world called... *drumroll*... <strong>Mysterious Bombo World</strong>!!! 🌍💥 Like, *what even is a Bombo*?! I don't know, but I am *here* for it!!! 
  </p>
</div>


<div style="background: #32CD32; padding: 15px; border: 2px dashed #FFD700; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0;">

    🐻 THE *~PLOT TWIST~* YOU DIDN'T SEE COMING 🐻
  </h3>
  <p style="color: #FFD700; font-family: 'Comic Sans MS', 'Comic Sans', 'Chalkboard', 'Chalkboard SE', 'Marker Felt', fantasy, sans-serif; line-height: 1.6;">
    So, like, the *wildest* part?! Cookie starts as a *human silhouette* in the opening cutscene (so *mysterious*, right?! 😱) but then *POOF* — she turns into a <strong>BEAR</strong> for the *rest of the game*!!! 🐻💖 And *plot twist*, besties — it's *NOT* just a random design choice!!! Our girl Cookie Blair is a *5-year-old from New Jersey* who *literally* *thinks* she's a teddy bear!!! 🧸💅 So of *COURSE* she looks like a bear in-game!!! It's *canon* and it's *perfection*!!!

  </p>
