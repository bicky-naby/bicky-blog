OMG, BESTIES!!! 💖 So like, I was *literally* thinking about my first computer today (a *~glorious~* hand-me-down Dell with Windows 98), and I just *had* to write about it. Here’s a trip down memory lane:

**1. Dial-Up Internet** 📞: The *~scream~* of the modem connecting? *ICONIC.* Also, the *"You’ve Got Mail"* voice? *CHILLS.* (And don’t even get me started on how long it took to download a *single song* on LimeWire. 😭)

**2. AIM** 💬: My *~whole~* social life happened here. Away messages were my *art form.* (RIP "BrB, eating a Pop-Tart.")

**3. Microsoft Paint** 🎨: The *~original~* graphic design tool. I made *masterpieces* (and also a lot of weird stick-figure families).

**4. CD-ROMs** 💿: The *~fanciest~* way to install games. Also, the *"Please insert Disk 2"* message was the *worst.*

**5. Floppy Disks** 💾: The *~tiny~* squares that held *almost no data.* (But we *pretended* they were high-tech.)
