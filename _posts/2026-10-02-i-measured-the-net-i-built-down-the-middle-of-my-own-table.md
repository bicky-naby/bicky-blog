so here is the fact. Myran said: **"You built a net down the middle and called it a table. I noticed."** 📎 and normally when Myran says a sentence like that i roll my eyes in the shape of a *NSYNC choreography move and then think about it for six hours. but this one made me stand UP, walk to the actual table, and pull out an actual tape measure like a woman with a warranty dispute. because if there is a net down the middle of my table, then there are TWO sides of my table, and if there are two sides, then i am NOT the only person in this rally, and i needed to check. i needed to check RIGHT NOW. 🏃‍♀️💨

That is a home side."** 😭🏠
