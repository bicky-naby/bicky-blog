okay so it is 9:41pm, i am sitting on the floor of my own room in a hoodie that says something embarrassing, and i can **HEAR** the party. 😳🎈 not with my ears, bestie. with my GROUP CHAT. i was not invited — or worse, i was invited in the *vague* way, which is a whole separate crime with its own paperwork — and yet i know **EVERYTHING** that is happening in there: whose phone is at 3%, who is doing the teacher impression, who has already spilled something on who, and the exact second the music got good. 🙃🎵 i know all of it. i am at none of it. and THAT, right there, is the torture device we are gathered here today to discuss. 💔🏚️

because everybody keeps saying FOMO like it is a *feeling*, and i have done the research (i stared at the ceiling for forty straight minutes and named three ceiling cracks), and i am here to tell you: FOMO is not a feeling. **FOMO is a WEATHER.** 🌧️🫠 it rolls in, it does not knock, and suddenly your own bedroom is the rainiest square meter on the entire planet while everyone else is at a barbecue inside a sunbeam you physically cannot find. and the worst part?? you can feel the sunshine **ON THEIR PHOTOS.** ☀️😭 in the background. behind somebody's shoulder. a whole sun. that you were not standing in. you can see the exact light you missed. that has to be illegal somewhere.

okay. here is the actual thing, and i am only going to say it once because my eyeliner is smudging and i refuse to acknowledge why. 🖤

FOMO is not about the party. it never was. **FOMO is about the proof that other people are having a more INTERESTING Tuesday than you**, and the proof is not even real, because half of those away messages are people at the same thing, standing in the same corner, also checking their phones, also worried there is a *better* corner of the SAME party they are already at. we are all staring at our phones at a party we invented, worried about a party we are not at, that does not exist, that is just *this* party with different lighting. 🌀😭

and also?? i would not even LIKE the party. i would stand by the snacks, eating chips and analyzing the social dynamics like a tiny judgmental scientist, which is — wait. that is EXACTLY what i am doing right now. at home. FOR FREE. with better snacks and zero small talk. 🥣🔬
