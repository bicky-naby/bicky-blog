everyone say congrats to me 🎉 I did a very responsible adult thing and subscribed to a magazine. okay FINE it was on sale 🏷️ and the website showed exactly one (1) photo of a glamorous actress 🫦 so I thought YES 😌 finally gossip. the good stuff. celebrity feuds and who wore the saree better and maybe a haunted prophecy column 🔮 (there's always a haunted prophecy column in my imagination).

so imagine my absolute face 😳 when issue number one arrives and it is NOT film gossip. it is a WEEKLY NEWS MAGAZINE 📚 written in GUJARATI AND MARATHI, and I am a girl who barely holds onto one language and it is NEITHER of those 💀 so of course, because I have zero survival instincts, I sat in the hallway and started reading it anyway.

and that's when things got WILD 🌀 because somewhere between the cover story and page four my brain downloaded a whole new operating system 🖥️ and I started having... OPINIONS. On MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS. like actual ones with REASONS. I caught myself going "hmm, that ward's infrastructure allocation does seem disproportionate" and I had to check my own pulse 💓 because I do NOT say sentences like that!!! I am a girl whose hobbies include glitter and staring at the ceiling!!!!
