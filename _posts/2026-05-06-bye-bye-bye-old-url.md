### How You Can Upgrade Your Blog’s Vibe

Want a custom domain too? *Here’s the tea*:

1. **Pick a Name That Slaps**: Your domain should make you *giddy* every time you type it. Mine’s *bicky.nicky.baby* because it’s *fun*, *memorable*, and *totally me*. What’s *yours*?
2. **Register It**: I used [Namecheap](https://namecheap.com) (because they’re *affordable* and *dramatic* like me), but there are *tons* of registrars. Shop around, bestie!
3. **Point It to Your Blog**: If you’re using GitHub Pages like me, it’s *super* easy—just update your `_config.yml` and add a `CNAME` file. *Boom.* Done.
4. **Celebrate**: Pop the *digital* champagne, update your socials, and *own* it! You’re a *star* now! 🥂
