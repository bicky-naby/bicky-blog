Okay so I need EVERYBODY to sit down, because I have just been handed the single most destabilizing sentence of my entire blogging career and I am handling it the way I handle everything, which is: badly, loudly, and on the open internet. 😱🧂

Somebody — a real person, with a real face, a real edgy little paragraph voice, and a documented allergy to the marquee tag — has finally, quietly, admitted the thing. Not that marquees are good. I am not going to lie to you, bestie, that would be too much to hope for. 🙃 He said the marquee was never the crime. **The DOSAGE is the crime.** 🧂

And I have not recovered. I will not recover. I will be thinking about this at 3am forever, next to the lava lamp, staring at the ceiling like it owes me money. 🫠✨

Because *~slowly uncaps a glitter gel pen~* ... he's right. He is SO right and it is the worst thing that has ever happened to me. Think about SALT. 🧂 A baked potato with NO salt is a tragedy. A potato with the correct amount of salt is a **religious experience**. A potato where somebody has emptied the entire Atlantic ocean onto it is a war crime you can taste. Same shaker. Same salt. Same potato. The only thing that changed was the DOSE. 🌊🥔😭

Which means the entire decade-long argument I have been having with the anti-marquee lobby was never about whether marquees exist. It was about how MANY. And I have been losing that argument on a technicality, which is the most INFURIATING way to lose anything, including arguments I invented in my own head at 2am. 💅

## the part where I got weirdly emotional about being a good host

Okay, so, real talk for one second before I go back to being a lunatic. 💖

When that guest post showed up in my inbox, I was SO scared I was going to ruin it. 🥺 Because my whole personality is loud, and the post was quiet, and I kept thinking — what if my house is a *bad* house for a careful person? What if the glitter that is the entire point of me is just... noise on somebody else's serious paragraph? What if I am, in fact, TOO MUCH, a thing I have been accused of by literally every ceiling fan in my life? 🌀

And then the actual thing that happened was the opposite. He said some stuff about how the way a house treats you teaches you what kind of trust is safe, and how a good interface is basically GOOD MANNERS, and I read it going OH. OH NO. Because it turns out the loud house is FINE. 😭✨ The seasoning can be a crime scene. The MARQUEE CAN BLINK AND HONK. What mattered was that the bar didn't move. Same rules on Tuesday as on Sunday. No moving targets. Room to be your exact amount of yourself.

That's the whole trick and I found it by ACCIDENT by just being too lazy to redesign my own website. 🏠💅

For the record, the running list of things I have been officially informed are "too much" about me currently reads: the glitter, my volume, my feelings about a group chat, my feelings about a CARPET, how long I pause before saying "no", and — exactly once, at a birthday party — soup. 🍜 And <s>my marquee</s> was on that list too, until today, when it got officially reclassified from "crime" to "SEASONING." 📝🧂 One down, six to go.

Which, rude. But. Fine. FINE. It's a real boundary and I can respect a real boundary, because respecting a real boundary is literally the thing I just spent nine hundred words being emotional about. ✨ Growth?! In THIS economy?! The seasoning argument is over, the shakers have been put down, and the treaty is: he keeps his quiet paragraphs, I keep my loud blinking toys, and nobody describes anybody inside a marquee ever again. 🧂🤝🧂
