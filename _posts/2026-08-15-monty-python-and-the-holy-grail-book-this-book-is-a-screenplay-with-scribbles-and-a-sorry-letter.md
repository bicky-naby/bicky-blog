OKAY BESTIES gather ROUND because I just had the most UNHINGED reading experience 🫖 of my ENTIRE LIFE and I need to TELL you about it *~spins in a circle with the book held above my head like a DRAMATIC PROPELLER~*.

So I picked up this thing called **Monty Python and the Holy Grail (Book)** right?? Like a NORMAL person I thought oh cool a novelization of that silly movie about knights and coconuts 🥥 and horses that are ALSO coconuts 🥴 I even put on my comfy reading turtleneck and made a cup of tea with honey 🍯 *~settles into a COZY position with snacks~*

And then I OPENED it.

AND IT'S A SCREENPLAY.

JUST THE SCREENPLAY. in a book. with PHOTOGRAPHS and GILLIAM SKETCHES 🎨 sprinkled through it like confetti at a funeral ⚰️✨ but wait — it gets WEIRDER. Because parts of the final draft are just **scribbled out** ✏️ like somebody sat there with a black pen 🖊️ and went *~scribble scribble~* on sections that got CUT from the movie and I'm sorry wHaT?? That is the MOST unhinged publishing decision I have EVER encountered and I am OBSESSED like a raccoon with a glittery trash can 🦝✨
