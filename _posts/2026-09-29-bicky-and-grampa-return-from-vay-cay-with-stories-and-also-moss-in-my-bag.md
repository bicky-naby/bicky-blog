### 1. the fishing incident 🎣

So Grampa decides he's gonna teach me how to fish 🐟 which is **CUTE** because i have the attention span of a gnat on espresso and i was **NOT** built for sitting still. He hands me a rod and starts explaining bait and hooks and i'm like **"yes yes i know how fish work"** and then i CAST and the hook gets stuck in a **TREE** behind us — and Grampa has to climb up there **he's 74 besties, he's climbing a TREE** 😱😱😱 to get it down. the sight of my 74-year-old grandfather scaling a pine tree because i couldn't control a fishing rod... **that's poetry**. that's oscar bait.

### 2. the night we saw a **BEAR** 🐻 (maybe)

Okay so it was like midnight 🌙 and we were sitting on the porch looking at the stars and we heard **THIS RUSTLING** in the bushes. Grampa goes **SHH** and turns off the flashlight and we're both just **STARING** into the pitch black woods like 👁️👄👁️ waiting for a **BEAR** to emerge and **EAT US** 🫣

And then it was a **RACCOON.** 🦝 A **fat** one. It looked at us, we looked at it, it waddled away with an **attitude** (literally flicked its tail, bestie, i saw it), and Grampa goes **"well that's dinner"** and i **SCREAMED** because i thought he meant **EAT THE RACCOON** and he **LAUGHED** until he coughed and said **"i meant we should've gone to that diner we passed"** 😭💀 i almost had a complete emotional breakdown over a raccoon that was probably heavier than me

### 3. grampa discovers **modern marshmallows** 🔥

Grampa bought a bag of **JUMBO** marshmallows at the gas station ⛽ and he was **SO EXCITED** to roast them. But these weren't the 1970s marshmallows he remembered — they were the **HUGE** gourmet kind that take like **TWENTY MINUTES** to toast properly. He held his over the fire for **8 seconds**, it caught **FLAME** 🔥🔥🔥, and he tried to blow it out but it just **MELTED** into a lava blob that dropped into the ashes 😂😭 the way his face just... **fell**. iconic. cinema.

**"These ain't right, Bicktoria,"** he said, holding up a stick with a blackened smudge on it. **"Back in my day a marshmallow knew its place."**

i bought him a regular bag the next day and he was **SO HAPPY**. the man toasted **FIVE** marshmallows in a row like a victory lap 🏆 he was in his element. he was **king of the campfire**. i have never seen a human being so emotionally connected to a bag of sugar puffs.

Meanwhile i burned **ALL** mine. Every single one. On **PURPOSE** because i **LIKE** them like that okay?? burnt marshmallow is a **TASTE** and it's **MY** taste and i will not apologize 🐷 you either get it or you don't and if you don't that's **your** spiritual failing not mine

### 4. the hike where i became **one with nature** 🧘‍♀️

Grampa wanted to take a "moderate" hike. **Besties.** **MODERATE.** We climbed what i'm pretty sure was a **SMALL MOUNTAIN** for **FOUR HOURS** ⛰️ and i was wearing the **WRONG SHOES** 👟 (converse bestie. i wore **converse**. to a mountain.) and also i brought a bag of chips that got **CRUSHED** in my backpack and i ate chip dust for sustenance like a feral creature 🫘 the way i had to lick the dust out of the bag corners... **that was rock bottom**. beautiful. transformative. covered in chip powder.

But the **TOP!!** 🏔️ the top of the hike was **SO BEAUTIFUL** 🌄 We could see the whole valley and there was this **WIND** that hit my face and i felt like i was in a movie 🎬 Grampa stood there with his hands on his hips (old man pose **ACTIVATED**) and said **"not bad for a couple of amateurs, huh?"**

And i said **"i'm not an amateur, i'm a PROFESSIONAL vacationer"** and he snorted so hard he almost lost his balance and i had to grab his arm and we almost **FELL OFF THE MOUNTAIN TOGETHER** 😱 and honestly?? i would've been okay with that as my origin story. **"Here lies Bicky and Grampa, they went out looking at a view and being dramatic."** ✨🪦✨ honestly that's a better epitaph than most people get
