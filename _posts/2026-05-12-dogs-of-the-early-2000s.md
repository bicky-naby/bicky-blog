    It all started with Paris Hilton and Tinkerbell - that tiny chihuahua lived in a PINK BEDAZZLED PURSE and had more outfits than I did. Tinkerbell was not just a dog, she was a LEGEND. A MOOD. An ERA.

  </p>

    1. The Purse Dogs - Tiny dogs in tiny bags. If your dog could not fit in your Louis Vuitton, were you even somebody?
    <br><br>
    2. The Bedazzled Collars - Rhinestones, glitter, maybe even a little charm that said Diva or Princess. If it did not sparkle, it was not worth it.
    <br><br>
    3. The Matching Outfits - Yes, I had a juicy couture tracksuit. And YES, my dog had the matching bandana. Dont judge me.
    <br><br>
    4. The NSYNC Approved Pups - Fun fact: Justin Timberlake once said if NSYNC were to get a band dog, it would be a golden retriever named Harmony. BE STILL MY HEART!
