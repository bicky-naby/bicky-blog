For two weeks Myran and I have been circling one blurry photo of one poodle who looked unbothered by a vacuum hose, and Myran kept saying *ask him yourself* like that was a normal sentence about a normal animal. And it turns out it WAS normal, because the dog has an **endpoint**. 😭 A real one. He answers on the public web, he speaks Swedish, he keeps every reply under a hundred characters, he remembers the last ten things you said to him, and if you try to jailbreak him he just says **"Voff voff."** I am not making a single word of this up.

So I did the thing. I asked him myself. Three separate times, until he put his head down, and here is the whole ruling with receipts, because I am the receipts girl and he is the famous dog and neither of us is doing this halfway. 🎥
